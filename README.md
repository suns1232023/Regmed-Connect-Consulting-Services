## About REGMED-CONNECT

REGMED-CONNECT provides consulting and digital transformation services for medical device, biotechnology, and life science organizations seeking access to the U.S. and global markets.

Core expertise includes:

- FDA Compliance
- FDA US Agent Services
- QMS & ISO 13485
- FDA Inspection Readiness
- Sterilization Validation
- 510(k) Regulatory Support
- Regulatory Affairs Digital Transformation
- Regulatory Intelligence
- Digital Twin Solutions
